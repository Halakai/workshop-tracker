Workshop Tracker: terms of use

Workshop Tracker is a private, non-commercial tool for the members of one car workshop. Accounts are created by the workshop’s admin only. Members use it to record their own workshop’s stock, cars, jobs and money, and are responsible for what they enter.

The app is provided as is, without any warranty. Keep your own backups (the admin can export all data at any time). See the privacy page for how data is stored.
